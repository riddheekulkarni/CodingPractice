Steps for preparation

To make white sauce pasta, start by boiling about 1½ cups of pasta (like penne, fusilli, or macaroni) in a large pot of water with a little salt and a teaspoon of oil. Boil until the pasta is cooked but still slightly firm (al dente), then drain and set it aside. While the pasta is boiling, you can prepare some vegetables if you'd like to add them—commonly used options include onions, capsicum, sweet corn, carrots, broccoli, or mushrooms. Chop the vegetables and lightly sauté them in a teaspoon of butter or oil for a few minutes until they are slightly soft but still have a crunch. Next, prepare the white sauce. In a pan, melt 2 tablespoons of butter on low heat. Add 2 tablespoons of all-purpose flour (maida) and stir continuously for a minute or two, making sure the flour doesn’t brown. Gradually add 2 cups of warm milk while stirring constantly to avoid lumps. Keep cooking and stirring until the sauce thickens. Once the sauce is smooth and creamy, season it with salt, black pepper, oregano, and red chili flakes as per your taste. If you want extra creaminess, you can add 2–3 tablespoons of grated cheese and mix until it melts into the sauce. Finally, add the sautéed vegetables and boiled pasta to the white sauce. Gently toss everything together so that the pasta is well coated with the creamy sauce. Cook it all together for another minute or two on low heat, and then turn off the flame. Serve the white sauce pasta hot, optionally garnished with more cheese or herbs, and enjoy it with garlic bread or toasted bread on the side for a complete meal.

Steps for preparation in points
